## Audio & Music
- **Soundtrack & SFX**: Synthesized dynamically in real-time in the browser using the Web Audio API (`AudioContext`).
  - *Synthwave Sunset Loop*: Procedural multi-track synthesizer with 16-step bassline, arpeggiated minor-ninth leads, punchy synthesized kick and snare drums, and ambient reverb delays.
  - *SFX*: Procedural FM synthesis, noise bursts, resonant filter sweeps, and pentatonic chime synthesis for jump, slide, orb collect, gem pickup, power-up surge, shield shatter, and crash.
  - Zero external `.mp3` or `.wav` dependencies. (License: CC0 1.0 Universal / Original)
